Prediction Model

How the engine scores and ranks numbers.

Model Overview

This site uses a weighted analytics engine to evaluate all 49 numbers using structural balance, long‑term frequency, recency momentum, and pair clustering.

The prediction engine assigns each number a score based on four major components:

  • Structural Patterns: odd/even balance, low/high distribution, decade spread, prime count, sum range, and consecutive pairs.
  • Frequency: long‑term hot/cold behaviour based on historical draw counts.
  • Recency: momentum from recent draws and streak behaviour.
  • Pair Clustering: numbers that frequently appear together or form consecutive pairs.

Each number receives a weighted score based on the selected mode:

  • Conservative: structure‑focused, favouring balanced numbers.
  • Hybrid: balanced structure + momentum.
  • Aggressive: momentum‑focused, favouring hot and recent numbers.

The top‑ranked numbers form the prediction sets shown on the dashboard.

Structural Patterns

How structural balance is measured.

  • Odd/Even: Balanced sets score higher.
  • Low/High: Even distribution across 1–24 and 25–49.
  • Decades: Spread across 0–9, 10–19, 20–29, 30–39, 40–49.
  • Primes: Ideal prime count is ~30% of the set.
  • Sum Range: Ideal sum is around 140, with acceptable range 100–180.
  • Consecutive Pairs: Fewer consecutive numbers score higher.
